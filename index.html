01 / Current work当前研究

Counterfactual video research反事实视频研究

In progress · Since July 2026进行中 · 2026 年 7 月至今

Can a model tell when a visual event stops making sense?模型能否识别一个视觉事件何时开始不合常理？

With guidance from Hokin Deng, I lead the project’s day-to-day development. We create paired videos with the same initial scene, changing whether the physical event unfolds plausibly.在 Hokin Deng 的指导下，我负责项目的日常统筹。我们从相同初始场景生成成对视频，控制事件是否符合物理规律。

I develop generators, define data and annotation standards, guide contributors through revisions, and run data-rendering and model-training experiments. I also built a shared review platform so assignments, feedback, and approvals no longer get lost in group chats.我开发生成器、制定数据与标注标准、指导成员修改，并开展数据渲染和模型训练实验。我也搭建了共享审核平台，让任务、反馈和审批不再淹没在群聊中。

Our ongoing experiments examine how training choices affect a model’s ability to locate violations without flagging physically correct events.我们正在研究不同训练选择如何影响模型定位物理错误的能力，以及它是否会把正常事件误判为错误。
